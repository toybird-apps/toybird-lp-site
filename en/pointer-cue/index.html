Frequently asked questions

Before you start

Is Pointer Cue for Mac free?

Yes. Pointer Cue for Mac is free to download. Highlighting the pointer, showing clicks, focusing the active window, and the white and red highlight colors remain free after the Pro Preview

What is the 60-day Pro Preview?

New users can use the complete Pointer Cue Pro feature set for 60 days from their first acquisition. After the preview, the app falls back to the Free feature set. You are not charged automatically

What does Pointer Cue Pro unlock?

Pointer Cue Pro lets you frame an area, configure the area-framing shortcut, and use additional preset and custom colors. It is a one-time purchase with no subscription

I purchased Pointer Cue before v1.3.0. Do I need to buy Pro?

No. Customers who purchased Pointer Cue before v1.3.0 receive Pointer Cue Pro at no additional cost

How is Pointer Cue different from a full annotation tool?

Annotation tools are designed for drawing and detailed markup. Pointer Cue is designed to guide attention while you continue interacting with the actual screen

Does it work with Zoom, Teams, and Google Meet?

Yes. Pointer Cue can be used while sharing or recording your screen, making it useful for online meetings, product demos, tutorials, and presentations

Is the Windows version the same as the Mac version?

No. The existing Windows version remains available, but active feature development is currently paused. The latest features and Free / Pro model are focused on macOS

Does it record typed text or screen content?

No. It does not collect, store, or transmit typed content or screen information
