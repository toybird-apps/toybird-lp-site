Frequently asked questions

What to know before you start

Start studying while your PDF is still processing

Import with the camera, photos, or a PDF and process pages in order. PDFs no longer have a fixed 20-page cap. Open ready pages, pause, resume, or retry an import. Free save slots, device storage, and document size still apply.

Two modes for different materials

Standard mode extracts key terms. The pre-marked-material mode uses existing red or orange text and highlighting in your material. It cannot recover text that is fully obscured in the original image.

Adjust masks in standard mode

Choose Low, Standard, High, or Before a test. Remove or add masks and switch patterns. Manual mask editing, density changes, and pattern switching are unavailable in pre-marked-material mode.

Free saves grow from 3 to 10

Start with three free saved pages. Complete your first study session to permanently expand the free limit to 10. Reserved slots and drafts from active imports also affect available capacity.

Are my materials uploaded?

Material images, recognized text, key terms, masks, and saved data are processed on your device. Study materials are not uploaded to external AI services or Toybird Labs servers. Exporting a backup or sharing sends data only to the destination you choose.

Which devices are supported?

iPhone or iPad with iOS / iPadOS 16 or later. iOS / iPadOS 26 or later can use newer document recognition. Background processing is used only on supported devices when permitted by the OS. Otherwise, keep the app open or return to resume processing.
