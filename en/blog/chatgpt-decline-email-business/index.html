How to Write a Polite Refusal Email with ChatGPT: Prompts and Examples

Create a clear, professional refusal email with ChatGPT by stating the request, the constraint, an alternative and the next action.

August 3, 20269 min readMasaki Iino

A professional refusal should be considerate without hiding the decision. ChatGPT can help with wording, but only when you clearly state what cannot be accepted, what can be offered instead and which facts must not be changed.

You will leave with a reusable prompt, a worked example and a short checklist for checking the result before you use it at work.

What you will be able to do

  • State the part of the request you cannot accept.
  • Give only the amount of explanation the recipient needs.
  • Offer a realistic alternative when one exists.
  • End with a clear question or next step.

Why a short instruction often misses the mark

A short instruction such as “Write a polite email declining this request.” can still produce fluent text, but it does not explain the business context, the reader, the limits or the required format. ChatGPT then has to fill the gaps, which is where generic wording and unsupported assumptions appear.

What to include before you ask

Before asking for the final answer, prepare the following information. You do not need to write a long prompt; each item only needs enough detail to change the result.

  1. Who the recipient is and your relationship
  2. The exact request you are declining
  3. The confirmed reason or operational constraint
  4. The alternative date, scope or condition you can offer
  5. The desired tone and any wording to avoid

Ready-to-use prompt

Replace the bracketed text with your situation. Remove or anonymise confidential information, and verify the final output against the source before using it.

Ready-to-use prompt
Write a professional email declining the request below.
Recipient: [client, supplier or partner]
Request received: [describe the request]
What we cannot accept: [scope, date, price or condition]
Confirmed reason: [brief factual reason]
Alternative we can offer: [realistic option]
Next action: [what the recipient should confirm]
Tone: calm, respectful and direct.

Do not invent dates, approvals or concessions. Create a subject line and email body.

Example: Declining unplanned work requested for this week

A partner asks for additional work by Friday. The team cannot complete it this week, but can deliver the work next Wednesday.

Subject: Timing for the additional work

Thank you for sending the additional request. Based on our current schedule, we will not be able to complete the work this week. We can complete it by next Wednesday.

Please let us know whether that timing works for you. Thank you for your understanding.

Ways to adapt the prompt

The core structure stays the same, but the facts, audience and desired output should change with the task.

Decline an earlier deadline

State the requested date and the earliest date that can be committed without reducing quality.

Decline a discount

Explain that the quoted price is final and offer a non-price alternative only if it is approved.

Decline a proposal

Thank the sender, state that the proposal will not move forward now and avoid criticising the product.

Checklist before using the result

A polished answer can still contain an incorrect fact or an unsuitable commitment. Use this checklist before sharing or sending it.

  • The refusal is explicit, not hidden behind vague wording.
  • The alternative is actually available and approved.
  • No apology implies legal liability or fault that has not been established.
  • Dates, prices and conditions match internal decisions.
  • The recipient knows what to do next.

Frequently asked questions

How much detail should I give about the reason?

Give enough context to make the decision understandable, but avoid unnecessary internal information or details about other customers.

How can I sound polite without creating false hope?

Use respectful language, then state the boundary clearly. “We cannot complete this by Friday” is clearer than “Friday may be difficult.”

What if there is no alternative?

Say so plainly, thank the recipient and explain the next relevant step, such as closing the request or revisiting it at a later date.

Can ChatGPT decide what concession to offer?

It can suggest options, but only a person with authority should approve a date, discount, scope or contractual change.
