How to Make ChatGPT Ask Clarifying Questions Before Answering

Use prompts that make ChatGPT ask for missing information one question at a time, in a short list or with selectable options.

Hands-on checks by Toybird Labs · official documentation referencedPublished: 2026-08-20Updated: 2026-08-24Reading time: 4 min

What this guide will help you do

  • Tell ChatGPT not to guess when essential information is missing.
  • Choose whether it should ask one question at a time or a short list.
  • Limit the questions to the information that changes the result.
  • Confirm the gathered facts before requesting the final output.

Ready-to-use prompt

State the task and what you already know. If a clarifying question asks for information you should not disclose, answer with an anonymised placeholder or say it is unavailable.

Ready-to-use prompt
I want to complete the following task: [task].
Known information: [facts you already have]
Do not make assumptions about: [dates, audience, policy, budget, etc.]

Before drafting the answer, identify the missing information that would materially change the result. Ask me one question at a time. Ask no more than five questions. When the necessary information is complete, summarise the agreed conditions and wait for my approval before drafting.

Example: Preparing a customer announcement

You know a feature will be released, but you have not decided the audience, release date, customer action or support contact.

Question 1: Who should receive the announcement: all customers, administrators only or users of a specific plan?

Question 2: Is the release date confirmed, and should the message include a time zone?

Question 3: Does the customer need to take any action before or after release?

Ways to adapt the prompt

Use one-at-a-time questions for complex briefs and a capped batch of questions when speed matters.

One question at a time

Best when the task is unfamiliar and each answer may change the next question.

Maximum three questions

Useful when you want to move quickly and only the highest-impact gaps matter.

Offer choices

Ask ChatGPT to suggest two or three options when you do not know the terminology or preferred format.

Checklist before using the result

Before acting on the final answer, check that the clarification step did not encourage you to disclose information you should not share.

  • Questions focus on information that changes the output.
  • ChatGPT does not ask for confidential data unnecessarily.
  • The number of questions remains manageable.
  • The final conditions are summarised before drafting.
  • You can stop the questioning and request a provisional draft when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Will ChatGPT always ask questions when I add this instruction?

Usually, but if it thinks the information is sufficient it may proceed. State clearly that it must wait for answers before drafting.

Can I ask for several questions at once?

Yes. Use a numbered list and set a maximum, such as three questions.

What if I do not know the answer?

Ask ChatGPT to present options and explain how each choice would change the result.
