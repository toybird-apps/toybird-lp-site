Review Your Worksheets Without Rewriting Them: From Import to Daily Recall

Add photos and PDFs, keep the original page layout, and return with Auto, suggested review and reminders. Build a reusable collection without turning every handout into a new card deck.

A new handout should not mean another evening of copying it into cards. Add the photos and PDFs you already use to AI Study Sheet, check the masks, and practise recall on the original page. As your collection grows, suggested review gives you a route back to material you have saved.

Add the material you already study

Start with a vocabulary page, a process diagram or a comparison table. Import a photo or PDF, then check the proposed masks. In the normal import mode, adjust masks where needed so headings, arrows and surrounding explanations remain useful.

You can add the next handout without rebuilding the earlier pages. Give materials names that help you find them, and use folders or search when you want a particular scope. Complete recall practice before spending time on a perfect filing system.

Keep the original page as context

A label in a diagram is easier to interpret when its arrow and surrounding structure remain visible. A term in a table needs its row, column and conditions. Mask the part you want to recall while keeping that context available.

When an answer looks familiar but you cannot explain it, return to the original text. Calculations and extended written answers still need practice in their original form.

Use Auto for a short recall session

Auto reveals highlighted answers after three seconds, reducing repeated taps to open each answer. Try to recall the answer before it appears, then compare it with the page.

In v4.1, let Auto adjust the number of masks or choose a fixed count from one to five. Auto adjust normally groups three and uses one or two at the end of a page or across distant areas. For dense pages, choose a smaller count or pause to read. Setting changes apply to new sessions; resuming keeps the settings used at the start.

Return to your collection with suggested review

Suggested review uses your self-ratings, learning activity and review timing to choose from saved materials. Select one to ten pages, three by default, or an estimated duration of three, five or ten minutes. A time estimate helps select pages at the start; it is not a countdown that cuts the session off.

When notifications are enabled and review material is available, a reminder can start Auto. After studying, rate how well you remembered the material yourself. These ratings are not automatic grading of your answers.

Try the whole flow before growing the collection

Import, check the masks, study, and return for another session using material you actually need. Completing your first study session expands the free save limit to ten pages, and Auto can be tried within that free allowance.

A one-time purchase removes the app’s page limit when you want to keep adding materials across courses or topics. The value is a collection you can continue using, with less time spent recreating pages or choosing the next page each time.
