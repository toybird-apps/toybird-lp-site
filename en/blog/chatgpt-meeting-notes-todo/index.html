How to Turn Meeting Notes into Action Items with ChatGPT

Use ChatGPT to separate decisions, action items, owners, due dates and unresolved questions from rough meeting notes.

Use ChatGPT to separate decisions, action items, owners, due dates and unresolved questions from rough meeting notes.

What this guide will help you do

  • Separate decisions from suggestions and open questions.
  • Extract an owner and due date only when the notes contain them.
  • Mark missing owners or dates as unconfirmed.
  • Keep a link or reference to the original notes.

Apply it to a real task

Start with the source of truth you already use—an email thread, meeting record, worksheet, PDF, or system screen—rather than creating another layer of notes.

Ready-to-use prompt
Organise the meeting notes below for follow-up.
Purpose: help participants confirm decisions and next actions quickly.
Output sections:
1. Brief summary
2. Decisions
3. Action items in a table: action, owner, due date, priority
4. Unresolved questions

Use only the notes. If an owner or due date is missing, write “Unconfirmed” instead of guessing. Merge duplicate actions but do not remove important context.

Example: Preparing a new service launch

The launch date is 20 August. The help page must be updated by 15 August by Tanaka. Final testing is due by 18 August, but the owner is not decided. The customer announcement date remains open.

Summary: The team confirmed the launch date and several pre-launch tasks.

Decisions: Launch on 20 August. Update the help page before launch.

Actions: Update help page — Tanaka — 15 August. Complete final testing — Owner unconfirmed — 18 August.

Open item: Decide when to send the customer announcement.

Ways to adapt the prompt

The core structure stays the same, but the facts, audience and desired output should change with the task.

Create a follow-up email

Turn the decisions and assigned actions into a short message to participants.

Prepare the next agenda

Use unresolved questions and overdue actions as agenda items.

Add tentative priority

Rank actions only when the notes contain enough impact and deadline information; label the ranking as a proposal.

Checklist before using the result

A polished answer can still contain an incorrect fact or an unsuitable commitment. Use this checklist before sharing or sending it.

  • Every action is supported by the notes.
  • Owners and dates were not invented.
  • Decisions are not mixed with proposals.
  • Duplicate wording did not hide separate responsibilities.
  • The original notes remain available for context.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT identify an owner from the conversation?

It can extract an explicitly assigned owner. If responsibility is only implied, ask it to mark the owner as unconfirmed.

Should I paste a full transcript?

You can, but remove unnecessary personal or confidential information and consider summarising very long transcripts in sections first.

How do I handle several meetings on the same topic?

Provide the date and source for each set of notes, then ask ChatGPT to keep current decisions separate from older ones.
