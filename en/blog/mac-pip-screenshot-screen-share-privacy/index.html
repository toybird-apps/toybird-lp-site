Separate what you can see from what the audience can capture

A screenshot, screen recording, shared window, shared tab, and full-display share can produce different results. Test the exact sharing mode before the meeting instead of assuming that one successful screenshot test covers everything.

In this guide, a PiP-style view means a separate live view of a selected supported app window, not only macOS video Picture-in-Picture. With Pocket Screen, the original app stays in place while the smaller live view remains above the workspace.

Frequently asked questions

Does exclusion guarantee that the view can never appear?

No. Operating-system and conferencing-app changes can affect capture behavior. For important sessions, verify the participant view from another device.

Is this the same as macOS video Picture-in-Picture?

No. macOS built-in Picture-in-Picture is mainly for video. Pocket Screen creates a separate live PiP-style view of a selected supported app window while the original app stays in place.
