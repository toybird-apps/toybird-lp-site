Use your own worksheets, textbook pages, screenshots, and PDFs for recall practice without rebuilding them as flashcards.

Decide what you need to recall before you take the photo

Do not treat the whole page as one memory task. Mark the terms, numbers, definitions, or diagram labels you must produce without looking. Keeping the original layout also preserves relationships between labels, examples, and diagrams.

Use your own worksheets, textbook pages, screenshots, and PDFs for recall practice without rebuilding them as flashcards.

Turn one page into a repeatable recall task

  1. Capture the page with even light and minimal perspective distortion
  2. Review the suggested terms and remove ranges that are not valid answers
  3. Answer aloud or mentally before revealing each mask
  4. Keep only the uncertain items for the next review

Worked example

Keep a labelled science diagram intact, hide only the terms you must recall, and reveal each answer after responding.

Return to the explanation instead of maintaining another card deck

A standalone card can lose the surrounding definition or diagram. The original page lets you move from a missed answer directly back to the explanation that made it meaningful.
