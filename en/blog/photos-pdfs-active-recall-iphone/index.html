Turn Photos and PDFs into Active Recall: From the Original Page to Auto

Import your own material, keep diagrams and explanations in context, and use Auto to recall answers. Add more pages over time and return through suggested review without rebuilding a card deck.

The handouts you already use can also be the pages you return to for recall practice. Add photos or PDFs to AI Study Sheet, check the masks, and study on the original layout. The example below shows how to preserve the clues that make an answer meaningful.

The example is instructional material, not a measured student result or a record of the app processing that page.

Start with a source you can check

Source Term Short explanation
R1 Evaporation Liquid water changes into water vapor.
R2 Condensation Water vapor changes into liquid water. Tiny droplets can form clouds.
R3 Precipitation Water falls from clouds as rain, snow or other forms of precipitation.

The useful distinction is a change of state versus water falling. A learner who swaps condensation and precipitation needs to return to R2 and R3, not merely look at the right word once. See USGS: condensation and USGS: precipitation.

Hide the answer, not the whole explanation

Keep visible Hide Why
“Liquid water changes into water vapor” Evaporation The state change is the clue being understood.
“Water vapor changes into liquid water” Condensation This distinguishes the reverse process.
“Water falls from clouds as rain or snow” Precipitation Falling, not just forming droplets, is central.

Hiding both the term and its explanation leaves no meaningful question. Leaving the same term in a nearby heading gives the answer away. Inspect the whole page, including captions, before deciding that a mask is sufficient.

Recall the answer before Auto reveals it

Use the visible explanation or diagram to recall the covered answer. Auto reveals the highlighted answer after three seconds, reducing repeated taps to uncover each one. Compare what you recalled with the original page, then read the explanation if the relationship is still unclear.

In v4.1, choose Auto adjust or a fixed count of one to five masks. Auto adjust normally groups three, with one or two at page ends or across distant areas. For a dense diagram, use fewer masks or pause to read. Changes apply to new sessions; a resumed session keeps its starting settings.

Import, check the masks, and begin with your own page

Import a photo or PDF of material you already study. In normal mode, check and adjust the proposed masks so row headings, arrows and relevant conditions remain visible. Correct recognition errors before repeating them in a study session.

Once the page works for you, add the next handout or chapter. Use a clear topic name and folders or search when you need a particular source. You can keep the page’s original context instead of writing each item into a new card.

Rate your recall and return to the right explanation

Rate how well you remembered the material after studying. This is your own assessment, not automatic grading. A missing term, a misunderstood arrow and a misread question need different follow-up: return to the definition, diagram or original exercise that explains the problem.

Suggested review uses self-ratings, learning activity and review timing, among other factors, to choose saved material. When notifications are enabled and review material is available, a reminder can take you into Auto.

Grow a collection you will actually revisit

Try the complete flow with material you need: import, check, recall, and come back. Completing the first study session expands the free save limit to ten pages. Auto can be tried within that allowance. A one-time purchase removes the app’s page limit when you want to keep adding materials.

For suggested review, choose one to ten pages, three by default, or an estimated three, five or ten minutes. The estimate selects pages at the start; it does not cut off the session at a time limit. Keep working through calculations and extended responses in their original form alongside these short recall sessions.
