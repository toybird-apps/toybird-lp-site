How to Summarize Long Documents with ChatGPT for Work

What this guide will help you do

  • Define who will read the summary and what decision they need to make.
  • Specify the sections and maximum length.
  • Require important numbers, dates and risks to remain traceable.
  • Ask for unsupported conclusions and missing information to be separated.

Apply it to a real task

Start with the source of truth you already use—an email thread, meeting record, worksheet, PDF, or system screen—rather than creating another layer of notes.

Ready-to-use prompt

Replace the bracketed text with your situation. Remove or anonymise confidential information, and verify the final output against the source before using it.

Ready-to-use prompt
Summarise the document below for [reader].
Purpose: help the reader [decision or action].
Maximum length: [words or reading time].
Required sections:
1. Main conclusion
2. Evidence and important numbers
3. Impact on our work
4. Risks or unresolved points
5. Recommended next actions

Use only the document. Preserve dates, figures and named conditions accurately. If the document does not support a conclusion, write “Not established in the source.”

Example: A three-minute summary for a department head

A long implementation report contains progress, delays, budget changes and decisions required from management.

Main conclusion: The launch remains possible this quarter, but approval is needed for the revised testing schedule.

Evidence: Two critical tests are incomplete. The budget impact described in the report is limited to the additional external review.

Decision required: Approve the revised test dates and confirm the owner for customer communication.

Ways to adapt the prompt

The core structure stays the same, but the facts, audience and desired output should change with the task.

Extract actions for the project team

Prioritise owners, due dates, dependencies and blocked items.

Explain to customers

Remove internal jargon and focus on customer impact, timing and required action.

Compare several documents

Separate common findings, disagreements and information that appears in only one source.

Checklist before using the result

A polished answer can still contain an incorrect fact or an unsuitable commitment. Use this checklist before sharing or sending it.

  • Important numbers and dates match the source.
  • The summary serves the named reader and decision.
  • Recommendations are clearly separated from source facts.
  • Omitted background does not change the meaning.
  • Confidential information is handled under the relevant policy.

Frequently asked questions

Can ChatGPT summarise a document that exceeds the input limit?

Split it into logical sections, summarise each section with the same criteria, then provide the section summaries for a final synthesis.

How do I prevent important details from disappearing?

List the items that must remain, such as dates, figures, obligations, risks and decisions.

Can I rely on the summary instead of reading the source?

For important decisions, no. Use the summary to navigate the source and verify the sections that support the decision.
