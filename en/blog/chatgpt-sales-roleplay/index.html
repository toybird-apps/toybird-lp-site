How to practice sales calls with ChatGPT: customer role-play and feedback prompt

Make the customer role realistic, keep the conversation interactive, and request evidence-based feedback after the role-play.

August 3, 202612 min readBy Masaki Iino

This guide shows a practical way to use ChatGPT at work. The goal is not to memorize a magic prompt, but to give the AI enough context, constraints, and an output format that match the task.

You can copy the prompt example, replace the bracketed details, and then review the result before using it in real work.

Key takeaways

  • Define the customer’s industry, role, situation, and concerns.
  • Tell the AI to ask one question at a time and stay in role.
  • Prevent coaching or scoring until the role-play has ended.
  • Set a realistic level of difficulty without making the customer irrational.
  • Use a fixed feedback rubric and examples from the conversation.

“Act as a customer” often creates an unrealistically easy conversation

Why a short instruction often fails

Act as a customer and practice a sales call with me.

A more reliable approach

Set the customer profile, buying stage, concerns, conversation rules, end condition, and evaluation criteria.

Information to provide

  • Customer industry, company size, and role
  • Sales stage and objective of the conversation
  • Business problem, priorities, and likely concerns
  • Rules for questions, objections, and when feedback is allowed
  • End condition and evaluation rubric

Reusable prompt template

Replace the bracketed parts with your situation. Ask the AI not to invent missing facts and to list questions separately.

Reusable prompt template
## Role
You are a [job title] at a [industry and company size] organization.
I am a salesperson presenting [product or service].

## Situation
- Stage: [discovery / proposal review / final evaluation]
- Customer goal: [goal]
- Concerns: [budget, adoption, security, migration, risk]
- What I need to learn: [needs, decision process, timing]

## Role-play rules
- Ask one question at a time, as in a real conversation.
- If my answer is vague, ask a follow-up question instead of giving me the answer.
- Stay in character and do not agree too easily.
- Do not coach or score me during the conversation.
- End after [8 exchanges] or when I type “End role-play.”

## Feedback after the role-play
Score the following from 1 to 5 and cite specific moments from the conversation:
1. Discovery of the customer’s problem
2. Clarity and relevance of the explanation
3. Accuracy and avoidance of unsupported promises
4. Handling of concerns and objections
5. Agreement on the next action

Begin with the first question.

Example: discovery call for a workflow tool

You are preparing for a first meeting with an operations manager who is considering a workflow tool but is concerned about adoption, migration effort, and security.

Practical example
Act as the operations manager of a 50-person company. This is a first discovery call, so prioritize questions about current workflow and impact before asking about features. Ask one question at a time. Be cautious about user adoption, migration effort, and security. Do not coach me during the role-play. After eight exchanges, evaluate discovery, clarity, accuracy, objection handling, and the next action.

Practical example

First question: What part of your current workflow causes the most delay or rework for the team?

The AI continues from your answer and saves the evaluation until the end.

Review before using the result

Treat the result as a draft. Confirm names, dates, prices, commitments, and company policy before sending or acting on it.

  • The role reflects a realistic customer and buying stage.
  • You asked questions before presenting features.
  • Unknown product details were not turned into promises.
  • The customer’s concerns were explored rather than dismissed.
  • Feedback refers to actual statements from the role-play.
Do not paste personal data, confidential customer information, contract details, or unreleased company information unless your organization allows it. Replace names and figures with placeholders when needed.

Frequently asked questions

Can I use voice mode for the role-play?

If your AI service offers voice conversation, the same role and rules can be used. Availability depends on the service and plan, so check the current interface.

How do I stop the AI from coaching me during the call?

Add: “Stay in role. Do not give advice, scores, or model answers until the role-play ends.”

How can I make the customer more challenging?

Add realistic constraints such as an incumbent vendor, limited budget, adoption concerns, or several decision makers. Do not simply ask the AI to be hostile.
