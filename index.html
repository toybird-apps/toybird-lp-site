Why the product pages and the blog live together

The apps and the articles are two sides of the same work. A product page explains what an app does. The blog is where we can write down the trade-offs, tests, and small workflow details that do not fit on a landing page.

We try to keep those roles separate: product claims belong on the product pages; longer explanations and comparisons belong in the guides. If a post comes from something we tested ourselves, we say so. If it depends on an OS or service specification, we check the official documentation.
