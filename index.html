Don’t let AI stay an idea

At Toybird Labs, we explore the practical potential of AI by actually building and publishing apps, games, videos, books, and articles.

We do not stop at concepts. We turn ideas into working products, publish them, and learn from how they perform in real use. This page highlights some of the projects that represent that approach.

Building reveals what theory misses

Some things about AI only become clear once you build and publish something real. What can be automated? What still needs human judgment? Where do quality control, operations, and distribution become difficult?

Toybird Labs turns those questions into small products and publishable content. This portfolio will continue to grow as more work is released.
